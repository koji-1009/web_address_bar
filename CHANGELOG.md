## 0.1.0

- Initial release.
- `WebAddressBar.enable`, `disable`, `isActive` and `collapsedExtent`. `enable` takes the `platforms` to take effect on, `WebAddressBar.supportedPlatforms` (Android and iOS) by default.
- `WebAddressBarScrollToTop`, which scrolls the primary scroll view in the foreground to the top on an iOS status bar tap.
